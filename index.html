Module 03

Crop disease scan

Take a clear photo of an affected leaf against a plain background. The scanner isolates the likely leaf area, normalises lighting, then reads colour and spotting patterns to suggest likely diseases.

📷

Tap to upload or take a photo

JPG or PNG · One leaf, good light, plain background

This scanner uses leaf-area segmentation plus colour/pattern heuristics; it is not a trained lab model. Treat the result as a first hint and confirm with your local Krishi Vigyan Kendra.

DATA & TRUST

Know what GROUND AID knows

Every major decision should distinguish retrieved data, cached data, modelled calculations and demonstration values.

🟢 LIVE

Retrieved from an API

Used only when a configured service responds successfully. The app records a timestamp and source.

🟡 RECENT CACHE

Previously retrieved

Shown when the network is unavailable and a previously fetched response is stored locally.

🔵 MODELLED

GROUND AID calculation

Scores, risk levels, advisories and economics derived from user inputs and available data. Not an official recommendation.

⚪ DEMO / SAMPLE

Placeholder values

Embedded demonstration records are never represented as live government or market data.
